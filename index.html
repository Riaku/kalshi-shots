Market rules Resolution criteria
  • Who asks for shots first? Resolves to the first named participant to verbally ask for shots (any phrasing: “shots?”, “who wants shots”, “shots o’clock”). Must be audible to at least two people.
  • Mentions in a group chat do not count, no matter how many exclamation marks.
  • Asking the bartender for a round of shots counts. Asking about shots, e.g. “do they do shots here”, does not.
  • Who’s gonna show up last? Resolves to the last named participant to physically arrive at the venue, as witnessed by a majority of people already there.
  • “On my way” sent more than 40 minutes before arrival is void and does not count as an arrival.
  • If two people arrive within 90 seconds, both resolve NO. Nobody wins. That is the joke.
  • Market resolves at 11:59 PM on the close date, or whenever everyone has gone home, whichever is later.
